ORIGINS / 2005 · DIGIPEN

Narbacular Drop.

Before there were test chambers, GLaDOS or Aperture Science, a student team called Nuclear Monkey Software created two connected portals in a living dungeon. Their 2005 puzzle game became the foundation for Portal.

Portal and its origins.

The PSBR FAQ describes December 2006 and retail Portal map sources, associated licensed development assets, and Portal Project Beta recreations. It notes that a genuine December 2006 prototype is not publicly released.

DIVISION 02 · F-STOP / CAMERA PROTOTYPE

The camera experiment.

F-STOP was Valve’s abandoned camera-based Portal experiment. After historical assets, source code, and map files circulated in 2026, community researchers assembled an unfinished but playable reconstruction. The original recovered material and later fan restorations are documented separately.

Before the gels.
