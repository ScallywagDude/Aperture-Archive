ARCHIVAL METHOD / IMPORTANT

TWO DIFFERENT CLOCKS.

Build date means when a historical version of a game was made. Leak or discovery date means when files became publicly known. The Portal 2 builds dated 2009–2011 surfaced much later as part of the 2026 Steam2 Teraleak. Those events appear separately so their chronology is not misleading.

Development-era records may have only an approximate month or year. Such entries are labeled accordingly. Original Valve material is separate from independent recreations; Exposure is the only community-project leak listed, because its build contained F-STOP-related code.
