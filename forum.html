COMMUNICATIONS PROTOCOL

Keep the record
useful.

  1. Search existing discussions before starting a new thread.
  2. Include the build date, steps to reproduce, and links to evidence where possible.
  3. Label rumors, fan reconstructions, and speculation clearly.
  4. Respect other community members and do not share private information.
