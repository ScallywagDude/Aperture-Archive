DOCUMENT 01 / DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

PROJECT HISTORY.

The original Portal: Prospekt appeared as a legacy demo in 2017. A substantially expanded demo followed in June 2025, bringing all 19 standard chambers and their advanced counterparts into a 2005-inspired industrial presentation. It reinterprets early visual language and is not an original Valve prototype.

HISTORICAL MILESTONES
  1. 2017

    Legacy demo documented on ModDB

  2. 2025-02

    Portal Project: 2005 development merged into Prospekt

  3. 2025-06

    19-chamber demo with advanced challenges published
