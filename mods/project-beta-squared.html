PROJECT HISTORY.

Project Beta² targets the early public preview era by reconstructing surviving presentation shots, portal effects, tests, and chamber geometry seen in 2006 demonstrations. Its historical source material and released recreations must be distinguished.

HISTORICAL MILESTONES
  1. 2006

    Portal preview demonstrations at Leipzig and X06

  2. 2024

    Project Beta² recreation material documented

  3. ONGOING

    Reference-based refinements and preservation work

DESIGN / MOTION / MEDIA RECORD

VISUAL ARCHIVE.

Where videos and concept breakdowns are published, the project's own website is the authoritative place to find them. The 2006 demonstrations are historical reference footage; recreated scenes are not original builds.

ARCHIVAL CLASSIFICATION

This is a community project dossier, not an official Valve prototype build. Its release or reconstruction date does not imply an original game-development date. Claims made by project authors are credited to their linked project records.
