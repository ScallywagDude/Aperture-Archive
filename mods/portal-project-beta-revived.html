DOCUMENT 01 / DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

PROJECT HISTORY.

Project Beta Revived packages separate interpretations of 2005 and 2006 Portal development. It makes the differences between industrial 2005 aesthetics and later 2006 testing facilities directly playable.

HISTORICAL MILESTONES
  1. 2005

    Recreated visual direction drawn from early Portal materials

  2. 2006

    Second era recreated from surviving reference

  3. 2019

    ModDB builds and optional bundled releases documented
