DOCUMENT 01 / DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

PROJECT HISTORY.

The Project-Beta team released early fan recreations before publishing a substantial full version in July 2017. The installation used original beta assets and maps where available, plus mod-specific restoration and scripting work. The project's archive also preserves its earlier 2009 demo, 2010 version, and standalone fixes.

DESIGN / MOTION / MEDIA RECORD

VISUAL ARCHIVE.

Source-map releases and dated mod builds allow the reconstruction's history to be compared. Beta-inspired visual material is a fan presentation, not proof of a separately leaked official 2006 executable.

ARCHIVAL CLASSIFICATION

This is a community project dossier, not an official Valve prototype build. Its release or reconstruction date does not imply an original game-development date. Claims made by project authors are credited to their linked project records.
