DOCUMENT 01 / DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

PROJECT HISTORY.

Portal Project: 2005 documented industrial test-chamber experiments and recreation work before its development was merged into Portal: Prospekt in February 2025. The project remains historically relevant because it explains the origin of design and mapping decisions visible in the newer Prospekt demo.
