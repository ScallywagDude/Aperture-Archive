DOCUMENT 01 / DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

PROJECT HISTORY.

Exposure began as a documentary-style archaeological project using camera-placement mechanics from Valve's abandoned F-STOP experiment. LunchHouse said it had obtained permission to demonstrate original code. The first public video appeared in late December 2019; January 2020 coverage explained the capture-and-rescale mechanic, and LunchHouse's Tristan Halcomb discussed plans for an episodic series. Community records later documented a January 10, 2023 Exposure development build. None of this is an official Valve F-STOP release; separate source/map discoveries enabled a different prototype reconstruction in 2026.

HISTORICAL MILESTONES
  1. 2007–2008

    Valve develops and shelves the F-STOP / Aperture Camera experiment

  2. 2019-12

    LunchHouse showcases the camera mechanic in the first Exposure video

  3. 2020-01

    International coverage of the documentary project and announced episodic plans

  4. 2023-01-10

    Unfinished Exposure development build dated in community archives

  5. 2026-11

    A separate reconstruction of Valve's own F-STOP prototype is documented; Exposure remains a different project

TEAM & ATTRIBUTION

WHO MADE IT?

LunchHouse Software is the team behind Exposure. Tristan Halcomb spoke publicly about its proposed documentary format and ongoing conversations with Valve. Credits to Valve's original F-STOP work and LunchHouse's subsequent documentary/recreation are kept separate.
