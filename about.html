Aperture Archive is a community idea inspired by the depth and dedication of Half-Life 2's beta-preservation scene.

OUR REASON FOR EXISTING

Some experiments deserve a second look.

Portal 2's history includes abandoned directions, altered mechanics, unused environments, visual experiments, and years of revision.

The archive brings fans, researchers, and modders together to document that history accurately, discuss the differences between development snapshots, and coordinate a proposed consolidated community Mega Build.

The model is familiar: a single community hub with news, curated documentation, releases, and conversation, in the spirit of Half-Life 2 Project Beta ↗. The subject is different, and this is a separate, independent project.

Dates and build references shown here link to existing historical documentation. Our collection and news are available now. Mega Build releases will follow as development progresses.

Project maintainer: @ScallywagDude ↗. Community conversations take place on GitHub Discussions.

Contribution priorities

  • Verifiable screenshots, art, and source citations.
  • Accurate build-by-build differences and test notes.
  • Reproducible fixes and respectful peer review.
  • Clear labels for original content versus fan-made reconstructions.
  • Clear research notes, contributions, and restoration history.
THE APERTURE ARCHIVE RULE

Label every specimen.

Original assets, mockups, and reconstructions must never be confused with one another. We do not use AI-generated imagery on this site.
