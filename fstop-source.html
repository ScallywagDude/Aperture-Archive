F-STOP SOURCE.

Historical F-STOP source material and original development maps, kept distinct from fan recreations and repairs.

ARCHIVAL CATEGORY2008Early experimental era · historical material

THE RECORD.

F-STOP was an experimental direction in Portal 2 development. Its surviving source and original map material are cataloged here under the early 2008 era. The date is a collection grouping, not a claim that every surviving file was created in 2008, or that a complete, playable F-STOP build leaked.
