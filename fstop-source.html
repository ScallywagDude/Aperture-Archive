F-STOP SOURCE.

Historical F-STOP source material and original development maps, kept distinct from fan recreations and repairs.

NOVEMBER 2026 / NEWLY RECOVERED EVIDENCE

THE PROTOTYPE RETURNS.

In late August 2026, a large historical Steam depot archive surfaced with most known F-STOP assets. Source code and map data surfaced separately in the following months. Community researchers assembled a largely playable version by November 1, 2026. The surviving prototype is unfinished and should not be mistaken for a complete or officially released Valve game.

ARCHIVAL CATEGORY2008Early experimental era · historical material

THE RECORD.

F-STOP was Valve's abandoned camera-based Portal prequel direction. The 2008 date groups early development materials, not every recovered file's creation date. Since the August–November 2026 discoveries, surviving assets, code, and maps have been assembled into a playable but unfinished community reconstruction. This archive distinguishes original prototype material from fan repairs and recreations.

THE ART OF F-STOP.

Original artwork, early Aperture visual research, and where to find the broader source collections. Artwork is labeled by provenance so that 2008 F-STOP concepts are not confused with later Portal 2 designs or community work.

ART RECORD / IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The Eric Kirchmer F-STOP concept sheet is identifiable as original F-STOP-era concept artwork. Other old-Aperture and 1950s imagery was developed or reused during Portal 2 production; that material is linked separately and is not automatically attributed to the cancelled F-STOP executable.

All seven numbered records are documented in the The Final Hours of Portal 2 image index. The standalone F-STOP sheet by Eric Kirchmer and the community reference collage remain separately credited below.
