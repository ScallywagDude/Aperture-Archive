What survives.

Historical source material and fan-made reconstructions are cataloged separately. A mod reproducing a beta does not make it an authentic leaked Valve build.

THE PSBR RESEARCH RECORD

The supplied Portal Series Beta Research FAQ describes December 2006 and retail Portal map-source files (VMFs), period textures and models, and licensee development materials. It describes a genuine December 2006 build used privately as a research reference, but not publicly released. The downloadable community projects below are reconstructions or derivative mod work.

ERA REGISTER.

2005

Industrial beginnings

The earliest visual direction is commonly reconstructed using Half-Life 2 textures, Nova Prospekt-style interiors and early chamber layouts. The exact features and dates are not all recoverable from public records; these are interpretations assembled from surviving map and asset material.

REFERENCE ↗
2006 / LEIPZIG

The first demonstration

Project Beta² reconstructs the Leipzig exhibition demo with older portal effects, lighting, signage and security cameras. Its description is based on footage of the public demonstration, not a publicly released original executable.

REFERENCE ↗
2006 / X06

Barcelona demo

The X06 material includes a different portal appearance and early sound, camera and indicator details. Project Beta² and the X06 addon preserve interpretations of this presentation.

REFERENCE ↗
DECEMBER 2006

The campaign takes shape

PSBR documents leaked VMF map sources, original period assets and licensed development content. Portal: Project Beta reconstructs the December 26 campaign from that material. PSBR says the genuine historical prototype is not publicly distributed.

REFERENCE ↗
FEBRUARY 2007

Closer to release

Project-Beta: Plus is a community reconstruction specifically targeting February 2007, a later stage than the commonly reproduced December 2006 maps. The mod author published an initial download in October 2026.
