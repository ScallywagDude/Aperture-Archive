ADDITIONAL RESEARCH / CHANGE REGISTER

WHAT CHANGED.

01 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Aquarium Core

PSBR describes the Aquarium Core in Jump Polarity as present in 852_6. Later December depots revised this part of the game.

02 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Ending and opera

Research exports describe the turret opera and outro sequence as still being assembled. Retail credits and choreography should not be back-projected onto this build.

03 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Caroline dialogue

852_6 scripts contain Caroline-related material that did not always play in the surviving campaign; absence of audio in a playthrough is not the same as absence from files.

04 / RESEARCH TOPIC

File identity

The user-supplied December GoFile mirror is documented as 852_6, NOT 852_7. This dossier and its video are for the first December revision.
