WHAT CHANGED.

01 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Depots stay separate

841_2 is the September 26 snapshot, distinct from 841_1 dated September 24. Its design and content should not be combined into a generic September build.

02 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Boss encounter changes

The recovered period shows a different Wheatley fight from retail, with altered presentation, triggers and incomplete sequences.

03 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Cave Cube research

PSBR comparisons describe Cave Cube content and related voice/script work around these September snapshots. Dependent files may not be identical between revisions.

04 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Nonfinal presentation

Temporary visuals and unfinished cutscene material reveal how late story beats were still being revised in fall 2010.

These notes draw on PSBR and VCC server discussions and associated historical footage, not a Valve changelog. See the dated Hidden Palace prototype record ↗ for depot identification. Related-era video is not proof that every scene came from this exact revision.
