NOTICE 001NOV 08, 2026PROJECT ANNOUNCEMENT

Introducing Aperture Archive

A proposed home for the parts of Portal 2 development history that rarely get their own spotlight.

Aperture Archive is a research and community hub for the proposed Portal 2 beta Mega Build. The aim is simple: gather documented information, keep provenance visible, make findings easy to compare, and give contributors a single place to discuss restoration work.

Our design takes its cues from Aperture Science's late-1960s and early-1970s visual language. The historical images shown on the site are sourced from real archival references, rather than AI-generated art.

Publication status: The collection is open, and the community is taking shape. The Mega Build will appear on the release desk when its first public version is ready.

DEPARTMENTAL INSTRUCTION NO. 3: Verify your sources. Label speculation. Never present a mock-up as an original.

Where to go next

Visit the collection to review build-era source links, read about the preservation mission, or use the message board to share findings and follow updates.
